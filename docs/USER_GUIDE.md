# ZAS Petroleum – User Guide

How to use the ZAS Petroleum Vehicle Services & Online Booking System on your Android phone and on the website.

## 2.1 About ZAS Petroleum

ZAS Petroleum lets you book, view and cancel vehicle service appointments from your phone or computer, without calling or walking in. The Android app and the website share the same account, so you can use either.

## 2.2 What you need

- An Android phone running Android 7.0 or newer (for the app), or any modern web browser (for the website).
- An internet connection.
- An email address and a cellphone number.

## 2.3 The mobile app

### Create an account

1. Open the app and tap Create an account.
2. Enter your full name (letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes only).
3. Enter your email address, for example name@example.com.
4. Enter your South African cellphone number, for example 0821234567 or +27821234567.
5. Choose a password: at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number. Tap Show to check what you typed.
6. Type the password again in Confirm password.
7. Tap Create account. You return to the login screen.

### Log in

1. Enter your email and password.
2. Tap Log in. The Home screen opens.

If the details are wrong you will see “Invalid email or password”. The Continue with Single Sign-On button is not available yet.

### Home screen

Home greets you by name and has two large buttons: Book a service and My bookings. Under Next booking you see your earliest upcoming booking and its status. The bottom bar switches between Home, Book, Bookings and Profile.

### Add a vehicle

1. Open the Book tab and tap + Add a vehicle.
2. Enter the make (for example Toyota) and the model (for example Corolla).
3. Enter the year, between 1980 and next year.
4. Enter the registration in the South African format, for example CAW123GP.
5. Tap Save.

### Book a service

1. Open the Book tab.
2. Choose your vehicle from the Vehicle list.
3. Choose a Service type. The price is shown next to the name.
4. Tap the Date box and choose today or a later date. Past dates are greyed out.
5. Tap an available time. Greyed-out times are already booked.
6. Add a note if you need to (maximum 200 characters).
7. Tap Confirm booking.

A box shows “Booking confirmed” and your reference number. Tap View my bookings to see it. If something is missing, a red message under that field tells you what to fix.

### View and cancel bookings

1. Open the Bookings tab.
2. Upcoming shows bookings still to come. History shows past and cancelled ones.
3. To cancel, tap Cancel on the booking, then tap Yes, cancel. Tap Keep it if you changed your mind.

Status colours: orange = Pending, green = Confirmed or Completed, red = Cancelled. To change the time of a booking, cancel it and make a new booking.

### Profile and settings

1. Open the Profile tab. Your name, email and phone number are shown.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Change your full name or phone number if needed. Your email cannot be changed.
4. Turn Notifications on or off for booking reminders and updates.
5. Turn Dark mode on if you prefer a dark screen.
6. Tap Save changes.

### Help and log out

Profile > Help has answers to common questions. Tap a question to open it. To leave the app, go to Profile > Log out.

## 2.4 The website

### Register and log in

1. Open the website and click Register (top right).
2. Enter your full name, email, cellphone number and password, then confirm the password, and click Create account.
3. Click Login and enter your email and password.

### Dashboard

After login you see a welcome banner with the number of upcoming services this week, a Book a Service bar, shortcuts to My Bookings and History, and your next appointments.

### Book a service

1. Click Book (or the orange Book a Service bar).
2. Choose a vehicle and a service type.
3. Pick a date. Available time slots appear; greyed-out times are fully booked.
4. Click a time, add Special instructions if you like (optional), then click Confirm Booking.

Red messages appear next to any field you missed, for example “Please select a vehicle.”

### My Bookings

Use the All, Upcoming and Past filters. Each booking shows a status badge (Pending, Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled). Click Cancel on an upcoming booking to cancel it.

### Settings

1. Click Settings in the top menu.
2. Under Profile, change your name or cellphone number and click Save profile.
3. Under Change password, enter your current password and a new one (8 or more characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number), then click Update password.
4. Under Notifications, switch Booking reminders, Status updates or Offers and news on or off.
5. Under Appearance, switch Dark mode on or off.

### Help and support

The Help page has a four-step quick start guide, common questions that open when clicked, and an Email support button if you are still stuck.

## 2.5 Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can’t reach the server | Check that your phone is online, then tap Try again. |
| No times shown for a date | The day may be fully booked. Choose another date. |
| Cannot pick a date | Past dates are disabled. Choose today or later. |
| “Use the SA format, e.g. CAW123GP” | Enter the registration without spaces: three letters, three numbers, then two letters. |
| Password rejected | Use at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number. |
| Still stuck | Contact ZAS Petroleum and quote your booking reference. |
